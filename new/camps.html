NCC Camps

Discover the key NCC camps that build leadership, discipline, and teamwork among cadets.

🚩 Republic Day Camp (RDC)

The prestigious RDC is held annually in New Delhi showcasing India's unity, discipline, and culture.

🏹 Thal Sainik Camp (TSC)

TSC tests cadets in obstacle training, map reading, firing, and endurance drills.

🎖 Combined Annual Training Camp (CATC)

CATC focuses on basic military training, discipline, and teamwork for cadets.

🪖 Army Attachment Camp (AAC)

AAC allows cadets to train with the Army, experiencing military life firsthand.
